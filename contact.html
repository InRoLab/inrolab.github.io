Work with us

Explore research collaboration and international mobility opportunities.

What we offer

We welcome postdoctoral researchers, PhD candidates, MSc and BSc students interested in robotic interaction, manipulation, continuum and soft robotics, and intelligent control.

  • Research supervision and scientific collaboration
  • Experimental and simulation-based robotics research
  • Joint scientific publications and conference contributions
  • Collaboration on research projects and grant proposals
  • Guidance on international research mobility and funding

How to join

Postdocs

Research opportunities may be supported through MSCA Postdoctoral Fellowships and other eligible funding programmes.

PhD candidates

Research visits and collaborative projects may be supported through Visegrad Fellowships, Erasmus+ and other eligible mobility schemes.

MSc & BSc students

Research internships and thesis-related activities may be supported through Erasmus+ or other eligible programmes, including DAAD RISE Worldwide for eligible Bachelor's students in Germany.

Contact

Interested in joining our lab or exploring research collaboration? Feel free to contact us with a brief introduction and your research interests.
