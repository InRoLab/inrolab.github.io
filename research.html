Research directions

Research in continuum and soft robotics, and intelligent robotic manipulation.

Research areas

Continuum Robotics

Modeling, design and control of continuum robotic systems for adaptive motion and physical interaction.

CONTINUUM ROBOTS

Soft Robotics

Development of compliant robotic mechanisms, soft actuators and grippers, with emphasis on sensing, modeling and contact-aware manipulation.

SOFT ROBOTS

Robotic Manipulation

Planning, control and learning for robotic manipulators operating under contact, uncertainty and changing environmental conditions.

MANIPULATION
